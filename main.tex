I focus on multimodal and neuro-symbolic methods, multi-agent collaboration for analysis and decision-making, and large-scale knowledge base construction and retrieval. Grounded in real-world industrial needs, my work targets transparent, controllable industrial agents and reasoning over multichannel industrial time series.
